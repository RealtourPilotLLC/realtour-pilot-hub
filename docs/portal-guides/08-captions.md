**Where it lives.** Each approved video has a caption box on its page. When a suggested caption is ready, it appears there, written from the video's script. You can also write your own.

**Edit it in place.** Your changes are saved with the video, so you can come back to it later.

**What makes a caption work.**

- **The first line matters most.** Most people only see it before they tap more. Make it the reason to keep reading.
- **Say one thing.** The caption backs up the video; it does not need to repeat it.
- **End with your call to action.** Tell people exactly what to do next: send a message, visit a link, book a call.
- **A few specific hashtags** beat a long list of general ones.

**Posting it.** Copy the caption from the video's page and paste it into the app when you post.
