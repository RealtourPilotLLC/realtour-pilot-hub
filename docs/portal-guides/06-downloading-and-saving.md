Downloads open once you approve a video. Tap **Download** on the video's page.

**On a computer.** The file saves to your Downloads folder. This is the most reliable way to get the full quality file.

**On an iPhone (Safari).**

1. Tap **Download**. Safari asks if you want to download the file; tap **Download** again.
2. The file goes to the **Files** app, in **Downloads**. It does not go straight into Photos.
3. To put it in Photos, open the video in Files, tap the share button, and choose **Save Video**.

**On Android (Chrome).**

1. Tap **Download**.
2. The file saves to your **Downloads** folder. Open it from the Files app. Many phones also show it in Photos or Gallery, under Downloads.

**If a download stops.** Videos are large files. Try again on Wi-Fi, or open the same page on a computer. Still stuck? Send us a message and we will get you the file another way.

**Keep the quality.** Always post the file you downloaded. A screen recording, or a copy that went through a text message or email, is lower quality.
