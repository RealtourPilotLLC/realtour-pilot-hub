**Post the downloaded file.** Upload the file you downloaded from the portal. Copies sent by text message or email are compressed and lose sharpness.

**Use Wi-Fi.** Apps often lower quality on mobile data to save your data plan.

**Turn on high quality uploads in Instagram.** In the Instagram app, open your settings, find **Media quality**, and turn on **Upload at highest quality**. The exact menu names change from time to time; if you cannot find it, message us and we will walk you through it.

**Do not re-export.** Each time a video is trimmed or saved again in another app, it loses a little quality. If you need a change, ask us.

**Vertical is ready to go.** Most of your videos are vertical (9 by 16), ready for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels without cropping.

**Give it a moment.** After posting, a video can look soft for a few minutes while the app finishes processing it.
