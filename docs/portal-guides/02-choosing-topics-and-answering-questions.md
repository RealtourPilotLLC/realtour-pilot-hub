Your topic bank lives under **Video Topics**. It is organised by the content pillars in your strategy, and we keep 10 to 15 ready topics in each pillar.

**Pick your topics.** Choose the ones you want for the month. The page shows how many your package includes and how many you have picked.

**Add your own ideas.** Anything you add is yours to use. It does not need our approval.

**Not for you?** Tap **Not interested**. You can tell us why if you like, and that topic will not come back. Changed your mind? Put it back from the set aside list.

**Answer the questions.** When you plan in writing, each topic has a few short questions. Your answers are what the script is written from, so specific beats long:

- a real example from your own work
- a number, a price or a timeline
- a short story about a client you helped

**If something is missing.** When an answer is too thin to write from, we ask one or two follow-up questions before we write the script. We only ask for things we do not already know about you.

**Already filmed.** Topics you have already filmed do not come back into your bank.
