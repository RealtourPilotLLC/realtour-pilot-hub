**Find it.** Open **My Videos**. Videos waiting on you are at the top.

**Watch it through once.** Then play it again and pause wherever you want something changed.

**Leave a note at that moment.** Each note is pinned to the exact second, so your editor sees exactly what you saw.

**Send your changes** once all your notes are in. They go to your editor together as one request.

**Approve** when it is right. Approving unlocks the download and the caption.

**Good to know.**

- Your package includes two rounds of changes on each video.
- We aim to turn changes around within one to two business days.
- Please review each video within four business days of it arriving.

**Changes go on the video.** Use **Messages** for general questions. Notes about a video belong on the video itself, so nothing gets lost between the two.
