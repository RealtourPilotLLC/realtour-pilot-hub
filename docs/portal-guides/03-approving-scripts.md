Every script has the same shape, so it is easy to read out loud:

1. a title
2. a strong opening hook
3. exactly three talking points
4. a close with your call to action

**Where to find them.** Scripts appear under **Video Topics** as soon as they are ready for you.

**Read it the way you will say it.** If a line does not sound like you, tell us. Nothing is too small to change.

**Approve** when it is right. You approve the exact version on your screen. If we update a script while you have it open, the portal asks you to read the new version before you approve.

**Ask for changes** and tell us what to fix. We revise it and send it back to you.

**Short on purpose.** Scripts are written to be short. If one looks long for the video it is meant for, we will point that out, and the decision is still yours.

Approve your scripts before your filming session so everything is ready when we arrive.
