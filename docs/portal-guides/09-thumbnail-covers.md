A cover is the still image people see on your profile before they tap play. A good one gets your video opened.

**Pick a strong frame.**

- Your face clear and your eyes open.
- A moment that matches the topic.
- Nothing blurry: pause and check before you choose it.

**Keep the important part in the middle.** Profile grids crop covers to a taller shape than the video, so keep your face and any words near the centre.

**Words on the cover.** A few words, large and high contrast, are easier to read on a small screen than a full sentence.

**Where to set it.**

- **Instagram:** when you post, tap **Edit cover** and slide to choose a frame, or add an image from your camera roll.
- **TikTok:** tap **Select cover** before you post and choose a frame.
- **YouTube Shorts:** choose a frame from the video when you upload.

The menu names change from time to time. If you cannot find the option, send us a message.
